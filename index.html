Kopek

Kopek is a fast, private expense tracker for people who keep money in more than one place — cash, bank, card, or a second currency for travel. Each is a wallet, and Kopek always knows what is in it.

Support

Found a bug, have a question, or want to suggest a feature? Email albagd+kopek@gmail.com. Mentioning your app version (shown at the bottom of Settings), iOS version and device model is usually what I need to reproduce a problem. You'll get a reply as soon as possible.

Common questions

Where do exchange rates come from? You enter them yourself, under Settings → Exchange rates. Kopek never fetches a rate, so nothing changes behind your back — a foreign-currency entry keeps the rate it was saved with. Totals that combine wallets in different currencies use your rates and are labelled as approximate.

How do I get my data out? Settings → Export CSV writes every transaction to a file you can share or save anywhere. Settings → Import CSV reads it back, and re-importing the same file detects the rows you already have instead of doubling your history.

I deleted something by mistake. Nothing is deleted immediately. Open Settings → Trash and tap Restore.

Is there a backup or sync? Not in this version. Your ledger lives on the device and is included in your normal iPhone backup. CSV export is the way to keep a copy of your own.

Your data

Kopek has no accounts and makes no network connections at all — everything you record stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
